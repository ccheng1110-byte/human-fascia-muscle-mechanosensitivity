# Step 15 computational strengthening amendment v1

## Status and scope

- This is a prospective computational-strengthening amendment created after Step 12C claim freeze.
- It does not replace, relax, or retrospectively modify the Step 10B/12C frozen panel, modules, thresholds, or prior CAUTION decision.
- New analyses are reported as perturbation triangulation or sensitivity analyses, not as independent fascia replication or causal proof.

## Primary strengthening question

In an external human fibroblast perturbation dataset, does a prespecified ECM/integrin-cytoskeletal program respond to mechanical tension after explicitly accounting for TGF-beta stimulation and age background?

## Staged plan

### Step 15A — GSE300230 provenance and feasibility audit

Audit official GEO metadata, biological-unit structure, mechanical/TGF-beta/age factors, processed-file availability, and frozen gene/module coverage. No biological hypothesis test is authorized at this step.

### Step 15B — analysis-contract freeze

Freeze the selected expression matrix, gene-symbol alias table, factor coding, exclusions, primary endpoints, model formula, multiplicity families, and fallback rules before examining condition-specific results.

### Step 15C — mechanochemical perturbation triangulation

Estimate mechanical tension, TGF-beta, age, and prespecified interaction effects at the biological-unit level. The primary mechanistic contrast is the mechanical effect after preserving the factorial design; cell lines/donors are the biological units.

### Step 15D — existing-data sensitivity package

Quantify cell-intrinsic versus composition-associated signal using sample-level summaries, then run the frozen scoring-parameter sensitivity grid and expression-matched negative-control gene sets. Cell-level p values cannot upgrade evidence.

### Step 15E — evidence resynthesis

Report separate evidence domains for observational association, perturbation plausibility, cross-tissue generalizability, fascia specificity, and causality. A positive public perturbation result does not by itself remove the CAUTION grade or establish fascia-specific causal mechanosensitivity.

## Authorized primary and secondary analyses

- Primary external perturbation source: GSE300230.
- Conditional replication source: GSE276045 only after GSE300230 is completed and only if its metadata audit supports a valid biological-unit model.
- Secondary regulatory analysis: prespecified TEAD/YAP, SRF/MRTF, SMAD, AP-1/NF-kappaB and hypoxia programs, with immutable gene-symbol alias handling.
- Sensitivity analyses: score-parameter grid, leave-one-biological-unit-out analysis, and expression-matched negative controls.

## Deferred analyses

- Fibromyalgia GWAS enrichment and SMR/TWAS are deferred from the current manuscript.
- Full FASTQ reprocessing is not authorized unless a later documented necessity cannot be addressed with processed matrices.
- F7/F8 scVI/scArches mapping may be exploratory but cannot close direct anatomical-equivalence or source-independence limitations.
- Human Protein Atlas expression may be descriptive supplementary context only.

## Integrity rules

1. No post hoc candidate replacement or threshold relaxation.
2. Gene aliases may correct nomenclature only; they may not substitute different biological genes.
3. Every external dataset must pass a provenance and design audit before expression testing.
4. Biological units, not individual cells or technical libraries, define inferential replication.
5. All null, discordant, and failed robustness results remain reportable.
6. Primary and exploratory findings must remain visibly separated.

## Material Passport

- Origin: Step 12C frozen claims, Step 14 manuscript package, and the reviewed dry-lab strengthening proposal.
- Transformation: the broad proposal was reduced to one primary perturbation source plus bounded sensitivity analyses.
- New expression values inspected at amendment creation: none.
- Evidence status: prospective amendment; prior evidence grade remains CAUTION.
