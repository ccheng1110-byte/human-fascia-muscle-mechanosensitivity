# Step 16A protocol-lock decision

Date: 2026-08-26  
Decision: **PROCEED_TO_PLATFORM_FEASIBILITY_CONFIRMATION**  
Current evidence grade: **CAUTION**

## Rationale

Step 15C–15E established a stable actomyosin-associated transcriptional response to mechanical tension in two cultured dermal fibroblast cell lines, but also identified proliferation as a competing response and could not estimate cell-intrinsic effects. The next informative experiment must therefore be donor-replicated, function-first, and perturbation-based.

## Locked scope

- Minimum three independent donors; four to six preferred.
- Donor-level biological replication; cells and image fields are nested observations.
- One prespecified mechanical challenge versus relaxed/reference condition.
- Vehicle/reference, integrin/focal-adhesion perturbation and Rho/ROCK–actomyosin perturbation.
- One locked primary functional mechanical endpoint.
- YAP/TAZ or cytoskeletal readout as orthogonal evidence.
- Viability, proliferation, ECM/TGF and inflammation controls in the same design.
- PIEZO2 reported as a secondary context-specific branch.

## Gate status

- Protocol lock: **PASS**.
- Laboratory feasibility confirmation: **PENDING**.
- Confirmatory data collection: **NOT YET AUTHORIZED** until platform range, reagent tolerability, primary endpoint, QC thresholds and blinding/randomization are documented.
- Evidence upgrade: **NOT YET EVALUABLE** because no new functional data exist.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

The planned experiment cannot be used to claim fascia specificity, pain causality, an independent age effect or universal PIEZO2 involvement. A positive result can upgrade mechanistic plausibility only if donor replication, alternative-state controls, perturbation dependence and orthogonal convergence are all satisfied.
